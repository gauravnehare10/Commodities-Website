> Interim stock photography until the business supplies its own photos (MI-10).

## Licence

All images are from **[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)** under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).

- **Allowed:** free commercial use; attribution is not required (credited here as good practice).
- **Not allowed:** selling the photos without significant modification, or compiling them into a competing image service.
- **Not used:** no "Unsplash+" (premium) images.

## Processing

- **Downloaded:** 2026-11-20, as 2400px JPEG originals.
- **Optimisation:** cropped with `sharp` (attention-based positioning) and encoded to WebP at quality 54–72, which keeps each image within the size budgets in UI_UX_GUIDELINES §5.
- **Sizes:**
  - Cards: 800×600, ≤ 120 KB
  - Wide page headers: 1600×700, ≤ 250 KB
  - Split and service images: 1000×800, ≤ 180 KB
  - Home hero: 1200×960 (5:4), ≤ 250 KB (re-exported from 1200×1400 when the desktop hero was made shorter)

## Selection notes

- Photos showing a real company's name or branding are avoided, so the site does not imply a partnership that doesn't exist. For example, a container ship with a visible shipping-line name was rejected for the Export image.
- The same photo is not used twice on one page. Repeats across different pages are acceptable.

## Replacing with client photography

To replace a stock image, keep its file name and dimensions and overwrite the file. No code changes are needed. Then remove its row from this table.
